Behind

Paige 18 is an independent production company based in Vancouver, developing original film, theatre and web series from first draft to final screening.

FRAME

We work with a small, trusted ensemble of writers, directors and performers — and keep every project close to the people who imagined it.

The Frame

#Vancouver#IndieFilm
#Theatre#WebSeries

We believe a story should be small enough to feel and big enough to carry — whether it lives on a stage, a screen, or a phone.

Since 2018 we have produced intimate, character-driven work across three disciplines, moving freely between rehearsal rooms and film sets. The same writers, the same care, a different frame.
