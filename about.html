Who we are

PAIGE 18 Production is an independent Vancouver studio making film, theatre and web series with a small, trusted ensemble of artists.

We develop original stories from the first draft and stay with them through rehearsal, production, post and release. Moving between the stage and the screen keeps our work honest: theatre teaches us to trust performers, film teaches us to trust the image.
